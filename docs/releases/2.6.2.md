# 4WP SEO Helper 2.6.2

**Published:** 2026-10-26  
**WordPress.org:** https://wordpress.org/plugins/4wp-seo-helper/

## Summary

SEO meta written by AI agents and bulk updates now show on the page in staging and development environments too.

## Fix

- **Yoast SEO:** Yoast renders title and description from its indexables table and saves indexables only in production mode. On staging / development the 2.6.1 rebuild ran but was not saved, so the page kept the stale (often empty) indexable and fell back to templates and an excerpt of the content. The rebuild now allows saving the indexable of that one post (`wpseo_should_save_indexable`, removed right after); the rest of the site keeps Yoast's behaviour.

## Existing sites

Pages edited with 2.6.0–2.6.1 on a non-production site keep the stale indexable until they are saved again (editor, quick edit or `update-meta`) or Yoast reindexes.
